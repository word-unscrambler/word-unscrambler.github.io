Announcing Our New Lightning-Fast Search Engine

Speed is the backbone of a great user experience, especially when you're in the middle of a high-stakes Scrabble game or a timed crossword puzzle. Today, we are thrilled to announce a ground-up rebuild of our core word-processing engine.

Related: Ready to test the speed? Head over to our Word Unscrambler tool and see the results instantly.

Why We Upgraded

Our previous engine was efficient, but as our dictionary grew to include over 450,000 English words, phrases, and technical terms, we noticed a slight lag on mobile devices. Our goal was simple: **Zero Latency.**

The new engine utilizes advanced bitwise operations and a trie-based data structure optimized for client-side JavaScript execution. This means the heavy lifting happens locally on your device, bypassing slow network requests.

Key Improvements

  • 400% Faster Processing: Average search time dropped from 45ms to under 10ms.
  • Reduced Data Usage: The new dictionary format is 60% smaller, saving your mobile data.
  • Offline Capability: Once loaded, the engine can unscramble words even if you lose your internet connection.
