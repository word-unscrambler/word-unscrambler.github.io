DESIGN INSIGHTS

Brutalist Design: Why We Chose High Contrast

In a world of soft gradients, rounded corners, and subtle shadows, our website stands out with sharp edges, heavy borders, and bold colors. This isn't just an aesthetic choice—it's a functional one. Welcome to the world of Neo-Brutalism.

See the Design in Action: Our Word Unscrambler tool uses this high-contrast interface to make finding words effortless, even on the go.

What is Brutalist Web Design?

Inspired by the architectural movement of the 1950s, brutalist web design emphasizes "honesty" in materials. In the digital space, this means using raw HTML-like elements, visible borders, and a clear hierarchy that doesn't hide behind decorative fluff.

For a utility tool like ours, this style allows us to strip away distractions and focus entirely on the letters you're trying to unscramble.

Function Over Form

The primary benefit of our high-contrast design is **accessibility**. By using a strict black-and-white base with vibrant highlights, we ensure that our tool is readable under any lighting condition—whether you're playing in a dark room or under bright sunlight.

Why High Contrast Matters for Word Games

When you are looking at a grid of letters or a long list of potential words, eye strain is a real concern. High contrast reduces the cognitive load required to distinguish characters, helping you spot that winning Scrabble word faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the design support Dark Mode?

Yes! Our brutalist approach translates perfectly to dark mode, swapping the high-contrast white for a deep black while maintaining the sharp borders and highlight colors.

Why are the borders so thick?

Thick borders (4px) create clear "containers" for information. This helps your brain categorize different sections of the page—like filters versus results—instantly.

Is this design mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Brutalist designs often use "blocky" elements which are much easier to tap and interact with on touchscreens compared to small, delicate buttons.
